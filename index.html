About

Hands on the hardware, head in the code

I'm Gunesh Gupta, a research intern at the Control and Autonomous Robotic Systems (CARS) Lab, IIT Roorkee. I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Bennett University (CGPA 9.23/10).

I build robots end to end: the mechanical structure, the electronics, and the ROS 2 software that plans, localises and moves them. My first robot from scratch was a 3-DOF arm controlled by Amazon Alexa. Since then I've built a 6-DOF PAROL6 arm with custom electronics, trained biped locomotion with RL at IIIT Hyderabad, and optimised manipulator morphology with RL at IIT Roorkee.
